# Directory artwork

The PNGs in `.wordpress-org` are the WordPress.org banner and icon, in standard and retina sizes. The release workflow publishes them to the SVN `assets` directory. They are excluded from the installable plugin ZIP.

The banner shows an illustrative sample blog. Its text and colors match the plugin defaults. Both designs were created with the built-in image-generation tool and exported to the [required WordPress.org dimensions](https://developer.wordpress.org/plugins/wordpress-org/plugin-assets/). The artwork is provided under the repository’s [GPL-2.0-or-later license](../LICENSE).

## Banner prompt

Create the finished header banner for the WordPress plugin "Corner Ribbon". Use case: ads-marketing / precise flat digital graphic. Target canvas 1544 by 500 pixels, panoramic ratio 3.088:1. This is a wide short website-directory banner, NOT a poster.
Black #080b0a background with restrained mint #00e49a accents. Crisp editorial typography, generous whitespace, exceptionally clean flat design. No gradients, glows, decoration, textures, 3D objects, photos, WordPress logos, or badges.
Left half: the exact title "Corner Ribbon" in large, beautifully spaced warm-white sans-serif typography on two lines, aligned left, with one smaller mint line beneath: "A tribute in the corner of your site."
Right half: an elegantly simple off-white sample blog window, flat and nearly front-facing, showing a black diagonal strip across its UPPER LEFT corner. The strip ascends from bottom left toward top right (/), with thin mint borders and exact mint text "R.I.P. ERIC" aligned along that diagonal. The strip spans the upper-left corner like the reference; it must not resemble an awareness loop or a bookmark. The sample page has a small heading "Field notes", a divider, and a larger article heading "In remembrance", with just a few subtle grey horizontal text lines below. Keep the ribbon prominently legible at half scale.
Use the provided screenshot solely as reference for the actual ribbon geometry and black/mint colors. Do not reproduce its personal name, navigation links, article titles, dates, or layout. This new sample blog is deliberately fictional.
Keep all important elements within a central 1440x430 safe area, with at least 48px margins. Only the specified exact text. No "RTIO", no watermark. The final output should be the banner itself filling the canvas edge to edge.

## Icon prompt

Create a finished square plugin icon for "Corner Ribbon", 1024 by 1024 pixels. Use case: logo-brand. A precise minimalist flat geometric symbol, designed to remain unmistakable at 128 pixels. Full square background #080b0a. Center a large warm-white rectangular page with lightly rounded corners, occupying about 76 percent of the canvas, with even generous padding. A broad black diagonal band crosses the UPPER LEFT corner of that page, ascending from bottom left to top right (/), bounded by two fine bright mint #00e49a lines. The band is clipped cleanly at the top and left page boundaries, like a corner tribute ribbon on a website. Leave the diagonal band empty: NO words, NO letters. In the lower right area of the white page show just three short, thin, grey horizontal lines suggesting text, with ample white space. Use flat fills, sharp clean geometry and consistent linework. No awareness loop, no bookmark hanging vertically, no bow, no folded 3D material, no gradients, no shadow, no texture, no extra icon, no border on the outer canvas, no watermark. The black/mint diagonal corner ribbon is the entire point of this symbol.
